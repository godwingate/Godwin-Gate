WHAT WE PROVIDE

Newsletter Examples

Explore four newsletter formats designed to help authors connect with readers and make their books easier to discover.

01 · WELCOME EMAIL

A warm first hello to new readers

Introduces the author, features the upcoming novel and invites readers to apply for advance copies, follow on social media and discover fellow authors.

  • Branded header & soft palette
  • ARC application call-to-action
  • Author recommendations

02 · RELEASE ANNOUNCEMENT

New book out, next book coming

Announces a new release alongside a pre-order, with retailer buttons, promotional graphics, event news and a reader giveaway.

  • Retailer & Goodreads buttons
  • Quote graphics
  • Event and giveaway sections

03 · SERIES LAUNCH

A complete series, in reading order

Celebrates the latest book in a series and guides readers through every title, the box set, the audiobook and Kindle Unlimited options.

  • Series reading order
  • Book-by-book links
  • Share & audiobook prompts

04 · MULTI-AUTHOR PROMOTION

A curated round-up of new reads

A themed collection that presents several books by genre, each with a cover, a short description and clear links for readers.

  • Genre sections
  • Consistent book listings
  • Elegant, minimal styling
About these examples

These examples show newsletter formats and presentation styles. Each project can be adapted to an author’s books, brand, audience, and promotional goals.
